The world

Explore

A coordinate world of towns, roads, wilderness and ruins. Each room describes what is there and where you may go next.

Talk and trade

Those who remain have their own accounts of what happened. Some will sell you what they can spare; some will ask something in return.

Stand your ground

Combat is turn-based and honest about its numbers. Factions remember whom you sided with.

The game is playable in English and Korean. Your choice of language changes every description, name and line of dialogue.
